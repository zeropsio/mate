# Crew work after a restart

When Mate restarts during crew work, the crewmate's row shows **Interrupted**, the last step that
finished, and **Continue**. Its edits stay in its crew copy. A running crew pauses so you can choose
what continues.

Press **Continue** on the work you want to carry on. For a check or a landing, this preserves the
copy's edits before checking the work again. If the work is already in Fen's code, Continue records
that outcome. **Drop it** cancels an interrupted task before landing and keeps its files in place.

A missing copy shows **Rebuild crew copy**. This rebuilds only that crewmate's copy from its saved
work. If its saved work is missing or has changed, the row explains why it stopped.

If a conversation has a different copy selected, it shows **Conversation points elsewhere** with
both paths. **Use crew copy** switches that conversation to its crew copy. Your selection stays in
place until you press it.
